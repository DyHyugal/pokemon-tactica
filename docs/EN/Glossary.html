Glossary

Short explanations for the strategy terms used in the wiki.

Setup
Using a turn to raise stats before attacking.
Support Pokémon
A Pokémon mainly used to help the team through screens, weather, status or recovery.
Entry hazards
Effects such as Stealth Rock or Spikes that punish Pokémon when they enter battle.
Pivot
A Pokémon or move used to attack and switch while keeping momentum.
Priority
A move that acts before ordinary attacks regardless of normal Speed order.
Wall
A very durable Pokémon used to absorb attacks.
Sweeper
A Pokémon designed to take several KOs, often after boosting its stats.
