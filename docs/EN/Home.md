# Home

Pokémon Tactica rebuilds Heart & Soul around strategy, team building and progression without unnecessary grind. NORMAL and HARD share the same content; HARD adds stronger optimization.

The wiki is a **player reference**:
- **Game Guide** explains systems and progression;
- **Changes** lists Tactica-specific modifications;
- **Pokédex** and **Locations** help build a team;
- **Bosses & Tips** explains opposing game plans without a turn-by-turn walkthrough;
- **Roadmap** tracks V1/V2 scope.
