Roadmap

V1 — validation phase

Pokémon Tactica's core is integrated: starter and Elm Egg flows, themed rival, progressive Team Rocket, Mega Evolution, strategic bosses, Level Caps, revised encounters, training tools, specialized shops and the black / red / gray interface. Johto, Kanto and the second League form one playable adventure.

The current phase is to build a candidate ROM from the latest integration/v1, complete a full playthrough and fix gameplay, balance or readability issues that are actually observed. Automated checks and green CI remain required before each new candidate.

After V1

Extensions that are not required for the first stable release remain planned for later, including Shiny Only, a complete French translation if it cannot fit cleanly in the single ROM, and deeper graphical redesigns.
