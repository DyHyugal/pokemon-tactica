GEN 2 REMAKE · JOHTO & KANTO

Pokémon Tactica

Take the road through Johto and Kanto again in a remake of the Generation II adventure built for players who want the nostalgia back without knowing exactly how the next battle will unfold.

Tactica keeps the journey, towns, characters and progression that made Gen 2 memorable, then pushes team building much further: a huge opening roster, completely revisited encounters, Gym Leaders built around real strategies and sixteen badges to claim before the toughest fights open up.

A familiar adventure, a different way to build

Tactica keeps the adventure and identity of Heart & Soul while changing how teams are built: reworked habitats, Level Caps, a persistent themed rival, strategic bosses, Mega Evolution, training tools and less unnecessary grind.

NORMAL or HARD

Both modes share the same content. HARD mainly adds stronger IV/EV optimization and more demanding AI.
