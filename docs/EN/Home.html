GEN 2 REMAKE · JOHTO & KANTO

Pokémon Tactica

Think you know Johto by heart? Good. Pokémon Tactica takes the classic Generation II adventure and turns it into a remake built to make every route, Gym and team-building choice feel worth discovering again.

The towns, characters and defining moments of Gen 2 are still here, but old habits will only get you so far: 30 starters + Eevee from the opening, a second choice through the Mystery Egg, completely revisited encounters, Gym Leaders built around real strategies, Mega Evolution in the heart of Johto and sixteen badges before the final challenges.

You know Johto. You do not know this version.

The journey keeps everything that makes Johto and Kanto instantly recognizable, then changes what matters when you build a team and step into battle. Routes offer far more options, bosses execute real game plans, and Tactica gives you the tools to test, adjust and perfect your six Pokémon without turning the adventure into a grind. The goal is not to replace your Gen 2 memories — it is to give you a reason to relive them with the sense of discovery you had the first time.

NORMAL or HARD

Both modes share the same content. HARD mainly adds stronger IV/EV optimization and more demanding AI.
