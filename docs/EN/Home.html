Pokémon Tactica

Rediscover Johto and Kanto through Pokémon Tactica. Pokémon habitats have changed, selected species have been strengthened or reworked, major battles are built around real strategies, and quality-of-life changes make progression smoother without removing the challenge.

A familiar adventure, a different way to build

Tactica keeps the adventure and identity of Heart & Soul while changing how teams are built: reworked habitats, Level Caps, a persistent themed rival, strategic bosses, Mega Evolution, training tools and less unnecessary grind.

NORMAL or HARD

Both modes share the same content. HARD mainly adds stronger IV/EV optimization and more demanding AI.
