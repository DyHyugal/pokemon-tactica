Credits

Project lineage

Pokémon Tactica is built from Pokémon Heart & Soul, itself based on several major open-source projects.

  1. pret / pokeemerald — Pokémon Emerald decompilation project;
  2. Modern Emerald by resetes12;
  3. Rom Hacking Hideout / pokeemerald-expansion;
  4. Pokémon Heart & Soul and its contributors;
  5. Pokémon Tactica, which adapts that foundation with its own gameplay, data and interface decisions.

Upstream credits and licenses must remain preserved in the repository and in any Tactica distribution.

Pokémon Heart & Soul

Heart & Soul provides the Johto foundation used by Tactica, including regions, progression, scripts, maps, GSC/HGSS mechanics adapted to the GBA engine, QoL systems, visual/audio foundations and pokeemerald-expansion integration.

Tactica is a fork built on top of that work and is not intended to obscure the origin of its components.

Tactica contributions

Contributions to code, data, documentation, testing, localization and assets should be credited according to their origin and applicable licenses.
