Changes

This page groups the gameplay changes specific to Pokémon Tactica. It is intended to remain an exhaustive V1 reference for changes that directly affect team building or progression.

Systems and quality of life

  • NORMAL and HARD use the same content; HARD optimizes IV/EV investment and AI.
  • Level Caps tied to major battles.
  • Persistent themed rival whose levels follow the next mandatory boss.
  • Training NPC for EXP, IVs, EVs/reset, nature, ability and friendship.
  • Native x1/x2/x3/x4 speed without speeding up music.
  • Independent music and SFX/cry volume.
  • Configurable Shiny Rate from 1/8192 to 1/1.
  • 30-starter selector plus Eevee and a second starter through Elm’s Egg.
  • Mega Ring obtained from Morty after Badge 4.
  • Dedicated strategic-item, move/TM and Mega Stone shops.
  • Custom interfaces aligned around black, red and gray.

Evolutions

Trade evolutions are adapted for solo play. Actual requirements come from compiled data; there is no universal 16/30/36 rule for every family. Wild encounters should also use an evolutionary stage coherent with their actual level.

Wild encounters

V1 uses 405 standard tables, 4 Headbutt tables and 53 Safari pools. Targeted tables use four real slots weighted 30/30/30/10.

The final pass prioritizes habitat coherence, progression, coherent evolutionary stages and maximum family availability before the first League. Unnecessary repetitions of the same base form across several routes are removed when they prevent another family from being available. No Legendary or Mythical Pokémon are inserted as ordinary random encounters.

Bosses, rival and Team Rocket

New rosters are shared by NORMAL and HARD; HARD adds optimization rather than different species. Falkner uses 3 Pokémon, Bugsy 4 and Whitney 6. The rival keeps its category and starter, then uses the level profile of the next mandatory boss. Rocket Executives use the latest relevant Gym/rival milestone +2, without chaining from the previous Rocket fight.

Mega Evolutions are part of selected boss fights. The player receives the Mega Ring after Morty and can use their own Megas immediately after Badge 4.

Shops

The move/TM NPC filters moves by compatibility. The strategic-item shop complements regular Poké Marts with relevant evolution and battle items without bypassing story items. Playable Mega Stones are sold by a dedicated NPC.

Interface

V1 keeps the HnS visual foundation while aligning custom interfaces around black / red / gray. Priorities are readability in the battle HUD, Summary, menus and shops. A deeper visual overhaul remains outside the V1 critical path.
