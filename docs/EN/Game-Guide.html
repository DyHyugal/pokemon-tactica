Guide

Traversal HMs are available once their story gates are unlocked: no learned move, HM item or compatible species is required. Interact with obstacles directly; Fly and Flash remain accessible from the party menu.

Not sure where to go next? This page follows the main Heart & Soul progression, adapted to Pokémon Tactica. It focuses on the next location and story event rather than listing every trainer or minor item.

Pokémon, levels, bosses and encounters come from Tactica. The HnS walkthrough is used only as a story-order reference. For team building, use Pokédex, Locations and Bosses & Tips.

Eevee

Start → Badge 1: Falkner

New Bark Town → Routes 29 / 30 / 31 → Violet City

In Professor Elm's lab, choose your first starter from the 30 Pokémon on offer plus Eevee. Cross Route 29 and Cherrygrove City, then head up Route 30 to Mr. Pokémon and receive the Mystery Egg. Fight your rival on the way back, then return to Elm's lab for the theft and police sequence.

This is where Tactica changes the story: when you identify the Mystery Egg species inside Elm's lab, you choose your second starter. This choice is not unlocked by the aide encountered later. Continue through Routes 30 and 31 to Violet City, clear Sprout Tower and defeat Falkner. Level Cap: 18.

Badge 1 → Badge 2: Bugsy

Violet City → Route 32 → Union Cave → Route 33 → Azalea Town

After Falkner, visit the Pokémon Center and complete the event with Elm's aide. It is a normal required progression event, but it does not unlock the second starter; that choice already happened in Elm's lab after the theft sequence. Travel down Route 32, cross Union Cave and continue over Route 33.

In Azalea Town, help Kurt and clear Team Rocket from Slowpoke Well. Defeat Bugsy. Level Cap: 25. Before leaving town, prepare for the rival battle guarding the way toward Ilex Forest.

Badge 2 → Badge 3: Whitney

Tactica cap: 32

Continue through Route 34 to Goldenrod City. Visit the Radio Tower and complete the quiz so Whitney returns to her Gym.

Defeat Whitney. Visit the Flower Shop afterward to obtain the item used on Sudowoodo on Route 36.

Badge 3 → Badge 4: Morty

Tactica cap: 38

Head through Route 35, National Park, then Route 36. Use the Goldenrod item on Sudowoodo and obtain Rock Smash. Continue via Route 37 to Ecruteak City.

Resolve the Rocket incident at the Dance Theater to obtain Surf. Explore the Burned Tower: rival battle and legendary-beast event. Defeat Morty. After the Badge and TM, he also gives the Mega Ring, making Mega Evolution available immediately before Badge 5.

Badge 4 → Badge 5: Chuck

Tactica cap: 45

Travel west through Routes 38 and 39 to Olivine City. Climb the Lighthouse and speak to Jasmine next to Amphy.

Surf across Routes 40 and 41 to Cianwood City. Obtain the SecretPotion, defeat Chuck, then obtain Fly.

Badge 5 → Badge 6: Jasmine

Tactica cap: 52

Return to the top of the Olivine Lighthouse and give Jasmine the SecretPotion. Once Amphy is healed, Jasmine returns to her Gym.

Defeat Jasmine.

Badge 6 → Badge 7: Pryce

Tactica cap: 57

From Ecruteak, head east through Route 42 to Mahogany Town. Go through Route 43 to the Lake of Rage and finish the Red Gyarados / Lance event.

Return to Mahogany and clear the Rocket Hideout. Defeat Pryce.

Badge 7 → Badge 8: Clair

Tactica cap: 64

After Pryce, return to Goldenrod City: Team Rocket has taken over the Radio Tower. Clear the Radio Tower, the Underground section with the rival fight, and finish the Rocket takeover.

Head east again: Route 44 → Ice Path. Obtain Waterfall during the crossing. Reach Blackthorn City, defeat Clair, then complete Dragon's Den to fully validate the eighth Badge.

First League

Tactica cap: Elite Four 67 · Champion 70

Return to New Bark Town for Elm's reward. Complete the mandatory Kimono Girls sequence in Ecruteak and the inherited HnS legendary story sequence that opens the League route.

Cross Route 27 / Tohjo Falls → Route 26 → Victory Road; the rival waits there. Enter the Indigo Plateau and defeat the Elite Four and Champion.

Kanto: eight more badges and a second conquest

Vermilion → Saffron / Celadon / Fuchsia → Power Plant / Cerulean → Pewter → Cinnabar → Viridian → Indigo Plateau

1. Arrival in Kanto — Lt. Surge

Tactica cap : 75

After the first League, collect the Ship Pass from Professor Elm and board the S.S. Aqua in Olivine. Once you reach Vermilion City, explore the city and defeat Lt. Surge to properly begin the Kanto campaign.

2. The center of Kanto — Sabrina, Erika and Janine

Tactica cap : 80

Head north to Saffron City for Sabrina, then travel west to Celadon City and challenge Erika. Continue toward Fuchsia City for Janine. By then, most of central Kanto becomes convenient to explore freely.

3. The Power Plant and Cerulean — Misty

Tactica cap : 85

Reach Lavender Town and Route 10 to access the Power Plant. The missing Machine Part leads to a Rocket sequence in Cerulean. Finish it, return the part to the Plant, then find Misty and earn her badge.

4. Radio, Snorlax and the northwest — Brock

Tactica cap : 90

Once power is restored, return to Lavender's Radio Tower for the radio access needed to wake Snorlax. Cross Diglett's Cave to reach Pewter City and defeat Brock. Route 3 and Mt. Moon also lead into another rival battle.

5. The southern islands — Blaine

Tactica cap : 95

Reach Cinnabar Island through southwestern Kanto, then continue to the Seafoam Islands for the battle against Blaine. Return to Cinnabar afterward to move the next Blue event forward.

6. Sixteenth Badge — Blue

Tactica cap : 100

After collecting fifteen badges, meet Blue again and travel to Viridian City. His battle is the sixteenth and final badge of the main campaign.

7. Back to Indigo Plateau

Tactica cap : 100

With all sixteen badges, return to Indigo Plateau. One last rival milestone stands before your second League. Win it, and the remaining endgame areas and challenges become your next destination.
