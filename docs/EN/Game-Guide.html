Game Guide

1. Difficulty

HARD is the default. NORMAL keeps the same teams, levels, moves, items, abilities and natures; HARD adds optimized IV/EVs and strategic AI.

5. Encounters and preparation

Each table has four species at 30/30/30/10. Fishing rods gate access without changing the species pool. The training assistant covers EXP up to cap, IVs, EVs/reset, nature, ability and friendship.

6. Bosses

Prepare multiple answers to the opponent's win condition. HARD AI can use weather, terrain, screens, hazards, pivots and setup without reading hidden player information.
