Game Guide

Pokémon Tactica is designed to let players build their own teams without forcing unnecessary grind. The wiki is a team-preparation reference: Pokédex + Locations to find a family, Changes for Tactica-specific edits, and Bosses & Tips to understand the opponent's game plan.

1. Difficulty: NORMAL and HARD

Both difficulties use the same content: the same boss rosters, species, sets, levels and mechanics. NORMAL keeps less optimized IV/EV scaling and AI. HARD adds the intended role-based IV/EV investment and more strategic AI.

The cap never moves backward. The rival follows the level profile of the next mandatory boss. Rocket Executives use the latest relevant Gym/rival milestone +2, without chaining from the previous Rocket fight.

5. Mega Evolution

Morty gives the Mega Ring after Badge 4 and his TM. Mega Evolution is therefore usable immediately, including the entire section between Badge 4 and Badge 5. A dedicated NPC sells playable Mega Stones.

6. Encounters and Pokédex

Main encounter tables use four real slots weighted 30 / 30 / 30 / 10. Distribution respects habitat, progression and coherent evolutionary stages.

When building a team: search the species in Pokédex, open Locations for area/method/level/rate, then check Changes if Tactica modifies its profile.

7. Training NPC

The NPC covers EXP up to the cap, IVs, EVs, EV reset, nature, ability and friendship. It removes grind without building the team for the player.

8. Shops and preparation

The move/TM NPC filters moves by compatibility. The strategic-item shop complements regular Poké Marts with relevant evolution and battle items. A separate NPC sells playable Mega Stones.

9. Reading a boss battle

Bosses & Tips lists the team, items, abilities, moves and overall plan. The goal is to identify the opposing win condition, the Pokémon that enable it, which answers you need to preserve and how to regain tempo — not to provide a turn-by-turn walkthrough.
