Start → Badge 1: Falkner

Tactica cap: 17
  1. In New Bark Town, configure your options, talk to Professor Elm and choose your Tactica starter.
  2. Route 29 → Cherrygrove → Route 30: reach Mr. Pokémon and obtain the Mystery Egg / Pokédex.
  3. Return toward New Bark: the first rival fight triggers on the way, then return to Elm's Lab.
  4. Travel back through Routes 30 and 31 to Violet City.
  5. Clear Sprout Tower, then defeat Falkner.

Badge 1 → Badge 2: Bugsy

Tactica cap: 25
  1. After Falkner, follow Elm's aide event at the Pokémon Center: in Tactica this sequence leads to the second starter through the Egg system.
  2. Go south through Route 32, cross Union Cave, then Route 33.
  3. In Azalea Town, talk to Kurt and clear Slowpoke Well.
  4. Defeat Bugsy.
  5. Leave Azalea to the west for the rival battle, then cross Ilex Forest and obtain Cut.

Badge 2 → Badge 3: Whitney

Tactica cap: 32
  1. Continue through Route 34 to Goldenrod City.
  2. Visit the Radio Tower and complete the quiz so Whitney returns to her Gym.
  3. Defeat Whitney.
  4. Visit the Flower Shop afterward to obtain the item used on Sudowoodo on Route 36.

Badge 3 → Badge 4: Morty

Tactica cap: 38
  1. Head through Route 35, National Park, then Route 36.
  2. Use the Goldenrod item on Sudowoodo and obtain Rock Smash.
  3. Continue via Route 37 to Ecruteak City.
  4. Resolve the Rocket incident at the Dance Theater to obtain Surf.
  5. Explore the Burned Tower: rival battle and legendary-beast event.
  6. Defeat Morty. After the Badge and TM, he also gives the Mega Ring, making Mega Evolution available immediately before Badge 5.

Badge 4 → Badge 5: Chuck

Tactica cap: 45
  1. Travel west through Routes 38 and 39 to Olivine City.
  2. Climb the Lighthouse and speak to Jasmine next to Amphy.
  3. Surf across Routes 40 and 41 to Cianwood City.
  4. Obtain the SecretPotion, defeat Chuck, then obtain Fly.

Badge 5 → Badge 6: Jasmine

Tactica cap: 52
  1. Return to the top of the Olivine Lighthouse and give Jasmine the SecretPotion.
  2. Once Amphy is healed, Jasmine returns to her Gym.
  3. Defeat Jasmine.

Badge 6 → Badge 7: Pryce

Tactica cap: 57
  1. From Ecruteak, head east through Route 42 to Mahogany Town.
  2. Go through Route 43 to the Lake of Rage and finish the Red Gyarados / Lance event.
  3. Return to Mahogany and clear the Rocket Hideout.
  4. Defeat Pryce.

Badge 7 → Badge 8: Clair

Tactica cap: 64
  1. After Pryce, return to Goldenrod City: Team Rocket has taken over the Radio Tower.
  2. Clear the Radio Tower, the Underground section with the rival fight, and finish the Rocket takeover.
  3. Head east again: Route 44 → Ice Path. Obtain Waterfall during the crossing.
  4. Reach Blackthorn City, defeat Clair, then complete Dragon's Den to fully validate the eighth Badge.

First League

Tactica cap: Elite Four 67 · Champion 70
  1. Return to New Bark Town for Elm's reward.
  2. Complete the mandatory Kimono Girls sequence in Ecruteak and the inherited HnS legendary story sequence that opens the League route.
  3. Cross Route 27 / Tohjo Falls → Route 26 → Victory Road; the rival waits there.
  4. Enter the Indigo Plateau and defeat the Elite Four and Champion.

Kanto → second League

Suggested main route
  1. After the first League, obtain the S.S. Ticket from Elm and sail from Olivine to Kanto.
  2. Vermilion City: defeat Lt. Surge.
  3. Reach Saffron City and defeat Sabrina.
  4. Continue to Celadon City and defeat Erika.
  5. Travel down to Fuchsia City and defeat Janine.
  6. Head through Lavender / Power Plant and complete the Machine Part quest.
  7. Return to Cerulean City, finish the Rocket event, then defeat Misty.
  8. Get the Radio upgrade in Lavender, wake Snorlax near Vermilion and cross Diglett's Cave.
  9. Reach Pewter City and defeat Brock.
  10. Continue through Route 3 → Mt. Moon for another rival battle.
  11. Reach Cinnabar Island / Seafoam Islands and defeat Blaine.
  12. With 15 Badges, speak to Blue on Cinnabar, then return to Viridian City for the sixteenth Badge.
  13. Return to the Indigo Plateau: rival fight, then the second League.

If you are stuck

Blocked route: resume from your latest Badge above. Fight too hard: open Bosses & Tips and use the Pokédex to find answers. Specific Pokémon: click its Pokédex card for the full ROM entry, or its Locations button to jump straight to encounter results.
