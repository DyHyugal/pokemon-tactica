# Changes

This page groups the gameplay changes specific to Pokémon Tactica. It is intended to remain **exhaustive** for V1 changes that directly affect team building or progression.

## Systems and quality of life

- NORMAL and HARD use the same content; HARD optimizes IV/EV investment and AI.
- Level Caps tied to major battles.
- Persistent themed rival whose levels follow the next mandatory boss.
- Training NPC for EXP, IVs, EVs/reset, nature, ability and friendship.
- Native x1/x2/x3/x4 speed without speeding up music.
- Independent music and SFX/cry volume.
- Configurable Shiny Rate from 1/8192 to 1/1.
- 30-starter selector plus Eevee.
- Second starter through Elm's Egg, different from the primary starter.
- Mega Ring obtained from Morty after Badge 4.
- Dedicated strategic-item, move/TM and Mega Stone shops.
- Custom interfaces aligned around black, red and gray.


### Difficulty and Level Caps

NORMAL and HARD keep the same Pokémon, rosters, sets, levels and mechanics. HARD mainly adds stronger IV/EV preparation and more demanding AI. The Level Cap follows the next major battle and never moves backward: Falkner 17, Bugsy 25, Whitney 32, Morty 38, Chuck 45, Jasmine 52, Pryce 57, Clair 64, Elite Four 67, Champion 70.

### Starters and Elm's Egg

The Johto trio is replaced by a selector with **30 starters across six categories + Eevee**. The rival keeps a coherent category and starter throughout the adventure. Elm's Egg then becomes a **second starter choice** and cannot give exactly the same species as the first choice. Required type boosters or intended evolution items are granted automatically.

### Settings and quality of life

Native speed offers **x1 / x2 / x3 / x4** without speeding up music. Music and SFX/cries use separate volume settings. **Shiny Rate** is configurable from **1/8192 to 1/1**. Settings remain grouped under Mode / Features / Randomizer / Nuzlocke / Difficulty / Challenges.

### Team preparation

The Training NPC covers **EXP up to the cap, IVs, EVs, EV reset, nature, ability and friendship**. Dedicated move/TM, strategic-item and Mega Stone NPCs let players prepare teams without unnecessary grind while leaving story items and progression events intact.

### Mega Evolution

Morty gives the **Mega Ring** after Badge 4 and his TM, so the player can Mega Evolve immediately throughout the section between Badges 4 and 5. Boss Mega users must start from a legal base form and legal base ability; the Mega ability is obtained through the transformation itself.

## Evolutions

Trade evolutions are adapted for solo play. Actual requirements come from compiled data; there is no universal 16/30/36 rule that applies to every family.

Wild encounters also respect an evolutionary stage appropriate to the actual level: a base form should not remain artificially present far beyond its normal evolution level.

## Wild encounters

V1 uses **405 standard tables**, **4 Headbutt tables** and **53 Safari pools**. Targeted tables use four real slots weighted **30/30/30/10**.

The final pass focuses on habitat coherence, progression, legal/coherent evolutionary stages and maximum family availability before the first League. Unnecessary repetitions of the same base form across several routes are removed when they prevent another family from being available.

No Legendary or Mythical Pokémon are inserted as ordinary random encounters.

## Bosses, rival and Team Rocket

New rosters are shared by NORMAL and HARD. HARD adds optimization, not different species.

Falkner uses 3 Pokémon, Bugsy 4 and Whitney 6. Later bosses use their canonical compositions listed in **Bosses & Tips**.

The rival keeps its category and starter, grows into fuller teams and uses the level profile of the **next mandatory boss**.

Rocket Executives use the latest relevant Gym/rival milestone **+2**, without chaining the previous Rocket fight's level.

Mega Evolutions are part of selected boss fights. The player receives the Mega Ring after Morty and can use their own Megas immediately after Badge 4.

## Shops

The move/TM NPC filters moves by Pokémon compatibility. The strategic-item shop complements regular Poké Marts with relevant evolution and battle items that should not depend on grind or unnecessary detours. Playable Mega Stones are sold by a dedicated NPC.

Story items, Balls, common healing and other normal progression items are not bypassed by these special shops.

## Interface

V1 keeps the HnS visual foundation while aligning custom interfaces around **black / red / gray**. Priorities are readability in the battle HUD, Summary, menus and shops. A much deeper visual overhaul remains outside the V1 critical path.
