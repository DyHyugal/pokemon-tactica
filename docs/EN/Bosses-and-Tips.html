Bosses & Tips

This page is the preparation reference for major fights: team, items, abilities, moves and overall game plan. NORMAL and HARD share the same content; HARD adds optimized IV/EV investment and more strategic AI.

Use the advice to understand the opposing win condition rather than as a turn-by-turn solution. “Dynamic” means the level is progression-calculated. Rocket uses the latest relevant Gym/rival milestone +2, never the previous Rocket fight.
