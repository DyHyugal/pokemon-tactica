Bosses & Tips

This page explains the battle plan behind major Tactica fights. Team compositions are shared by NORMAL and HARD; HARD adds optimized IV/EV investment and advanced AI without turning the wiki into an automatic solution. Exact teams may still receive small balance changes during the final playthrough.

Johto

Falkner — Flying / Tailwind

Theme: Speed control through Tailwind, then momentum with Taunt and U-turn.

Danger: trying to outspeed everything while Tailwind is active.

Tip: stall a few turns, use resistances and keep priority for weakened targets.

Avoid: leaving a slow frail Pokémon in front of an already boosted attacker.

Bugsy — Bug / hazards

Theme: Stealth Rock + Spikes followed by offensive pressure.

Danger: repeatedly switching while hazards keep stacking.

Tip: stop the lead from getting several free layers or clear hazards early if your team can.

Avoid: forgetting that a lead which has finished its job may look for an explosive sacrifice.

Whitney — Normal / Baton Pass

Theme: build an advantage and pass it to a dangerous receiver.

Danger: giving Eevee several free setup turns.

Tip: break the plan before Baton Pass through Taunt, phazing, direct pressure or a quick KO.

Avoid: waiting until the boosted receiver is already on the field before reacting.

Morty — Ghost / burns and setup

Theme: cripple physical attackers, then create setup turns for Ghost threats.

Danger: relying entirely on physical damage with no answer to burns.

Tip: keep at least one special threat or a reliable way to avoid giving Will-O-Wisp free value.

Avoid: handing a free setup turn to Mimikyu, Annihilape or another sweeper.

Chuck — Fighting / hyper offense

Theme: immediate pressure, quick setup and chained KOs.

Danger: losing tempo through an overly passive turn.

Tip: preserve priority, a fast revenge killer or a healthy Fighting resist.

Avoid: giving free setup to a Pokémon that is already difficult to outspeed.

Jasmine — Steel / hazards + defensive pressure

Theme: hazards, bulky pivots and Pokémon that can turn Defense boosts into damage.

Danger: letting Ferrothorn establish hazards, then trying to break the whole team only with physical attacks.

Tip: exploit special weaknesses and stop bulky setup Pokémon before they stack boosts.

Avoid: unnecessary switching through hazards with no removal plan.

Pryce — Ice / Snow + Aurora Veil

Theme: Snow, Aurora Veil, then setup opportunities for the rest of the team.

Danger: trying to win a normal damage race while Aurora Veil is active.

Tip: pressure Alolan Ninetales quickly or stall out Veil before giving Baxcalibur/Piloswine free turns.

Avoid: treating Piloswine as disposable middle-stage filler; it is intentionally the ace.

Clair — Dragon / multiple win conditions

Theme: paralysis, Dragon Dance, fast pressure and a crit-focused Kingdra plan.

Danger: allowing Focus Energy or Dragon Dance to go unanswered.

Tip: keep a healthy Kingdra answer and avoid letting Speed control open the door for the sweepers.

Avoid: sacrificing your best Dragon answer too early.

Elite Four

Will — Psychic Terrain offense

Theme: Psychic Terrain, screens and strong special breakers.

Tip: pressure Indeedee and the screen setters. Once screens expire, trades become much more manageable.

Avoid: giving multiple Calm Mind turns behind dual screens.

Koga — Poison / status + Speed

Theme: hazards, poison, sleep, pivots and fast cleaners.

Tip: limit status early and preserve an answer to the fast late-game threats.

Avoid: letting Amoonguss repeatedly reset through Regenerator for free.

Bruno — Fighting / wallbreakers

Theme: very high physical damage with few passive turns.

Tip: Fighting resists and Attack control are valuable. Breaking his momentum is often better than trying to win a long defensive battle.

Avoid: underestimating guaranteed No Guard attacks or short setup windows.

Karen — Dark / screens + cleaners

Theme: screens, paralysis and finishers that become stronger after KOs.

Tip: remove or stall out screens and avoid feeding easy KOs to Honchkrow or Kingambit.

Avoid: sacrificing several weakened Pokémon in sequence and creating a snowball.

Proton — tempo and sacrifice

Theme: pivots, burns, chip damage and Explosion after support duties are complete.

Tip: do not give the pivots free value and be ready for sacrifices that create safe entries for another attacker.

Petrel — disruption

Theme: situational Ditto, Illusion, HP reduction and explosions.

Tip: avoid overboosting while Ditto can still copy the setup, and confirm what you are actually facing before committing.

Ariana — status + momentum

Theme: paralysis, sleep, Encore/Parting Shot and late-game sweepers.

Tip: reduce the free turns created by status and exploit moments where support Pokémon do not threaten an immediate KO.

Archer — mixed offense

Theme: breakers, fast pivots and broad coverage.

Tip: do not look for one universal wall; identify which member of your team answers each breaker and keep those answers healthy.

Kanto

Lt. Surge — Electric Terrain

Theme: Electric Terrain and Alolan Raichu's Surge Surfer.

Tip: changing or stalling out the terrain removes a major part of the team's pressure.

Avoid: trying to simply outspeed Alolan Raichu while terrain is active.

Erika — Grassy Terrain

Theme: sustain, boosted Grass attacks and Contrary Serperior pressure.

Tip: do not let Leaf Storm boosts stack for free. Keep an immediate answer to Serperior.

Avoid: playing too passively under Grassy Terrain.

Misty — Rain

Theme: Rain offense, Swift Swim and amplified Water damage.

Tip: change the weather or stall out Rain turns before committing your main checks.

Avoid: racing Swift Swim users under active Rain.

Janine — Poison stall

Theme: Toxic Spikes, recovery, Haze and Regenerator.

Tip: clear hazards/status where possible and force the defensive core to stay in instead of freely recycling health.

Avoid: sending in a boosted sweeper without accounting for Haze.

Sabrina — Trick Room

Theme: slower Pokémon move first for a limited number of turns.

Tip: count Trick Room turns, use priority and pressure the setters instead of trying to win the normal Speed race.

Avoid: spending several Trick Room turns setting up yourself.

Brock — Sand

Theme: Sand, hazards and Sand Rush Excadrill.

Tip: change the weather or stall out Sand. Once Sand Rush is gone, the fight becomes far more manageable.

Avoid: giving Excadrill a free Swords Dance with several Sand turns remaining.

Blaine — Sun

Theme: Sun offense, boosted Fire attacks, Chlorophyll and instant Solar Beam.

Tip: changing weather has huge value. Otherwise preserve a strong Fire answer and deny free setup.

Avoid: relying on a Water resist that becomes much weaker under Sun.

Blue — Hyper Offense

Theme: immediate pressure, pivots, breakers and setup threats.

Tip: play for tempo. Passive turns are rarely free against Blue; preserve your resistances and priority to regain momentum after a KO.

Avoid: trying to stall the entire team without a way to take initiative back.

Champion

The Champion teams are owner-locked: they reflect the intended Tactica design and are not automatically replaced by external competitive sets.

First battle — Dragon offense

Theme: Speed, pivots and several sweepers that can exploit a single free turn.

Tip: preserve Dragon/Fairy/Ice answers and do not allow repeated Dragon Dance opportunities.

Avoid: giving the lead free momentum and then sacrificing your checks one by one.

Kanto rematch — mixed offense

Theme: Jolteon applies pressure and pivots with Volt Switch; multiple sweepers then take over before Mega Metagross.

Tip: a Ground type can cut the Electric pivot line, but Jolteon is expected to hard-switch rather than stay trapped. Use that forced switch to regain tempo.

Avoid: forgetting Quick Attack: it is intentionally retained as low-HP priority even on Modest Jolteon.
