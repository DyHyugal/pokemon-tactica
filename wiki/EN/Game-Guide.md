# Game Guide

## Difficulty

Pokémon Tactica is designed to start on **HARD by default**. A New Game disclaimer explains that **NORMAL** remains available.

### HARD

HARD uses Tactica boss teams: stronger synergy, sensible items, IV/EV investment, weather, terrain, screens, Trick Room and recognizable win conditions.

### NORMAL

NORMAL uses the same Tactica boss teams, sets, levels and content as HARD, but keeps NORMAL IV/EV scaling and NORMAL AI. It is a less optimized Tactica experience, not vanilla Heart & Soul.

## Settings

The Tactica setup menu is organized into six tabs:

- **Mode**
- **Features**
- **Randomizer**
- **Nuzlocke**
- **Difficulty**
- **Challenges**

Recommended applies the intended Tactica defaults. Custom opens the detailed settings.

V1 also targets **B = Save & Exit** from any settings tab.

## Level Cap

The Level Cap follows progression toward the next major mandatory battle. It is intended to prevent overleveling from replacing strategy.

The cap should never move backward once progression has advanced.

Rocket Admin battles use a dynamic rule: every Pokémon is set to the last canonical Gym Leader/Rival milestone cap +2; a Rocket battle does not become the reference for the next Rocket boss. Evolution legality is still respected.\n\nThe Training NPC reads the live cap directly and can raise a Pokémon up to that limit.

## Starters

The Tactica starter selector offers six type categories plus Eevee:

- Fire
- Water
- Grass
- Electric
- Ground
- Ice
- Eevee

The rival receives a starter from a category designed to answer yours.

After Elm's Egg quest, the player chooses a **second starter** through the same Tactica system.

### Starter items

Each typed starter receives its type booster. Any required evolution item is added on top.

Eevee receives three rewards after choosing an Eeveelution:

1. Silk Scarf;
2. the Eeveelution's type booster;
3. the corresponding evolution stone.

## Training NPC

The Training NPC reduces preparation grind. Services include:

- IVs;
- EVs;
- EV reset;
- EXP;
- friendship;
- nature;
- ability.

It does not build a team for you; it simply speeds up preparation.

## Native game speed

Game speed can be set to **x1 / x2 / x3 / x4**.

This is native logical acceleration: music keeps normal playback speed, and sensitive scenes can remain at x1 where necessary.

## Audio

Music and SFX/cry volume are independent:

- MUTE
- 20%
- 40%
- 60%
- 80%
- 100%

## Special shops

Tactica separates TMs into:

- Support;
- Weak;
- Powerful;
- Ultimate;

and strategic items into:

- Evolution;
- Strategic;
- Other/QoL.

Special shops are not intended to replace normal Poké Mart progression.
