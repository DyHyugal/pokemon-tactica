# Home

**Pokémon Tactica is a Generation II remake built around Johto and Kanto.** Johto’s roads are open once more, but the adventure has been rebuilt around strategy: broader family access, Gym Leaders designed as real team battles, modernized progression and sixteen Badges to conquer.

The towns, characters and defining moments of Gen 2 are still here, but old habits will only get you so far: 30 starters + Eevee, a second choice through the Mystery Egg, reworked encounters, Level Caps, a themed rival, Mega Evolution and tools for building teams without unnecessary grind.

The wiki is the **player reference**:
- **Guide**: concise progression help when you are unsure where to go;
- **Changes**: everything Tactica changes from the HnS base;
- **Pokédex**: ROM-specific species and family details;
- **Locations**: areas, methods, levels and rates;
- **Routes & Cities**: progression, services, items and encounters area by area;
- **Bosses & Tips**: teams, sets and game plans;
- **Roadmap**: V1/V2 scope.

NORMAL and HARD share the same content. HARD mainly adds stronger IV/EV optimization and more demanding AI.
